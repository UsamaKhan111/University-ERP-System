# Presentation Slide Outline

## Slide 1: Title
Smart University ERP System

## Slide 2: Problem
Universities need centralized student, teacher, course, attendance, exam, result, fee, and analytics management.

## Slide 3: Tech Stack
React, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB Atlas, Mongoose, JWT, Zod.

## Slide 4: Database Design
Show users, students, teachers, courses, enrollments, attendance, exams, results, and fees.

## Slide 5: Relationships
One-to-many and many-to-many relationships through ObjectId references.

## Slide 6: Role-Based Access
Admin, teacher, and student permissions.

## Slide 7: Aggregation Pipelines
Department counts, attendance trends, revenue analytics, GPA analytics, and defaulter lists.

## Slide 8: Backend Architecture
MVC, services, validators, middleware, distributed modules.

## Slide 9: Frontend Screens
Dashboard, students, teachers, courses, attendance, exams/results, and fees.

## Slide 10: Testing and Deployment
Jest, Supertest, Vitest, Vercel, Render/Railway, MongoDB Atlas.
